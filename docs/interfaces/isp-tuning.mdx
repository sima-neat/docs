# ISP Tuning

The image signal processor (ISP) of MLSoC Modalix turns the raw data of a camera
sensor into the image that an application receives. Getting frames from a
camera and getting good images from it are two separate steps. This page says
what tuning is, which cameras are already tuned, and how to get the tuning
guide.

:::caution[Not yet validated on hardware]
This page summarizes the other camera pages of this site and the kernel source
for platform release 2.1.3. The camera and ISP owners have not reviewed it yet.
:::

## Capture and Tuning {#capture-and-tuning}

| | Capture | Image-quality tuning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Result | The board lists the camera, and the camera delivers frames | The frames have the right exposure, white balance, and color |
| What it needs | A sensor driver and a device-tree overlay | Tuning data for the sensor and its lens |
| Where to start | [MIPI Camera Interfaces](../getting-started/standalone-mode/mipi-camera-interfaces.mdx) | The Modalix Camera & ISP Tuning Guide |

The ISP is an Arm Mali-C71.

## Cameras That Are Already Tuned {#tuned-cameras}

The platform release is tuned for the cameras in
[Supported MIPI Cameras](./supported-cameras.mdx): the Arducam IMX477, the
e-con Systems IMX568 and IMX678, and the Leopard Imaging AR0234 and OG05C10.
With one of these cameras, you do not need to tune the ISP to get started.

## When You Need Tuning {#when}

- You use a sensor that is not in the list above. See
  [Integrate Your Own Camera](./custom-camera.mdx).
- You use a supported sensor with a different lens or camera module.
- The image quality of a supported camera does not meet the needs of your
  application.

## Get the ISP Tuning Guide {#tuning-guide}

The Modalix Camera & ISP Tuning Guide describes how to tune the ISP for a
camera. It is available on request. See
[Reference Documents](../reference/extra-docs.md).

To ask SiMa.ai about tuning a camera, contact your SiMa.ai representative, or
use the [SiMa.ai contact page](https://sima.ai/contact/).
