# Integrate Your Own Camera

Use this page when your camera is not in
[Supported MIPI Cameras](./supported-cameras.mdx). It shows what has to be true
before a camera can produce frames on a Modalix board, what you have to write,
and how to check each step. A camera that is not on that list is not supported.
Expect driver and device-tree work, and separate work on image quality.

:::caution[Not yet validated on hardware]
This page is written from the kernel source for platform release 2.1.3. Nobody
has integrated a new camera by following it.
:::

## Check the Fit First {#check-fit}

| Question | What the platform provides |
| --- | --- |
| MIPI CSI-2 lanes | Each CSI interface of the Modalix SoM has two data lanes. A camera that needs four lanes on one interface does not work. |
| Interfaces | The SoM has four CSI interfaces, CSI0 to CSI3. A carrier board connects some of them. The Modalix DevKit connects CSI1 and CSI2. |
| Connector and cable | The connector on the camera must reach the connector on the carrier board. You may need an adapter. |
| Control bus, clock, power, and reset | The sensor needs an I2C bus, a reference clock, its supplies, and usually a reset or power-down pin. The carrier board decides which pins provide them. |

For the camera connectors and signals of a carrier board, see
[Carrier Board Bring-up](../design/som-carrier.mdx).

## Check the Driver {#driver}

The kernel must have a driver for the sensor. Platform release 2.1.3 enables
drivers for these camera sensors: Sony IMX219, IMX415, IMX477, IMX568, and
IMX678, onsemi AR0234, and OmniVision OG05C10. It also enables the GMSL
serializer and deserializer drivers MAX96714, MAX96717, MAX96724, and MAX9296A.
The list is in
[`simaai_modalix_defconfig`](https://github.com/SiMa-ai/simaai-linux/blob/65aba29989da02d7612da5484dbbcd50643fdd60/arch/arm64/configs/simaai_modalix_defconfig).

For another sensor, add its driver to the kernel. See
[Build the BSP from Source](../software/build-from-source.mdx).

A driver in the kernel does not make a camera supported. The cameras that have
an overlay are in [Supported MIPI Cameras](./supported-cameras.mdx).

## Write the Overlay {#overlay}

Start from the overlay of a supported camera for your carrier board and port,
and change it for your sensor. For example, see
[`modalix-som-waveshare-ARDU-IMX477-1CAM.dtso`](https://github.com/SiMa-ai/simaai-linux/blob/65aba29989da02d7612da5484dbbcd50643fdd60/arch/arm64/boot/dts/simaai/overlays/modalix-som-waveshare-ARDU-IMX477-1CAM.dtso).
The overlay has to do these things:

1. Provide the reference clock of the sensor.
2. Enable the I2C bus that the sensor is on.
3. Set the power-down or reset pin of the camera.
4. Enable the CSI interface of the port, with its D-PHY and video nodes. For
   port 1, these are `csi1`, `mipi_dphy_rx1`, `axi_vdma1`, `v4l2video1`, and
   `v4l2vdma1`.
5. Describe the sensor: its `compatible` string, its I2C address, and its clock.
6. Connect the sensor to the CSI interface, with `data-lanes = <1 2>` and the
   link frequency of the sensor mode.

To build, install, and check the overlay, see
[Device Trees and Overlays](../software/device-tree.mdx).

## Validate Capture {#validate}

1. List the cameras. Your camera must be in the list:

   ```console
   sima@modalix:~$ cam -l
   ```

2. List the formats and sizes that the camera offers:

   ```console
   sima@modalix:~$ cam -c 1 -I
   ```

3. Capture a frame:

   ```console
   sima@modalix:~$ cam -c 1 --capture=1 -F -s pixelformat=NV12
   ```

If the camera is not in the list, see
[If It Does Not Work](../software/device-tree.mdx#if-it-fails).

## Image Quality Is a Separate Step {#image-quality}

A camera that captures frames does not yet produce good images. The ISP needs
tuning data for each sensor and lens. In platform release 2.1.3, the ISP driver
in the kernel has sensor entries for the IMX477, IMX568, IMX678, and OG05C10
only. For another sensor, plan for ISP tuning. The Modalix Camera & ISP Tuning
Guide in [Reference Documents](../reference/extra-docs.md) describes it, and is
available from your SiMa.ai representative.

## Get Help {#help}

To ask SiMa.ai about integrating a camera, contact your SiMa.ai representative,
or use the [SiMa.ai contact page](https://sima.ai/contact/). Include the sensor
and the part number of the camera module, the carrier board and the port, the
platform release, and the result of each validation step.
